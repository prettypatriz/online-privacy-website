INTRODUCTION TO ONLINE PRIVACY

Online privacy is the protection of personal information while using the internet.

Learning about online privacy helps us protect our personal information and use the internet safely.

ONLINE PRIVACY SAFETY TIPS

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Do not share personal information publicly.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check your privacy settings.
  • Be careful when using public Wi-Fi.
