ONLINE PRIVACY

Protect Your Digital Life

Online privacy is the protection of your personal information while using the internet. It helps prevent your information from being misused or shared without your permission.

What is Online Privacy?

Online privacy means having control over the personal information you share online, such as your name, location, photos, passwords, and other private information.

Common Privacy Risks

  • Identity theft
  • Data breaches
  • Online tracking
  • Phishing and scams
  • Oversharing on social media

How to Protect Your Privacy

  1. Use strong and unique passwords.
  2. Enable two-factor authentication.
  3. Be careful when clicking links.
  4. Check your privacy settings.
  5. Avoid sharing sensitive information publicly.

Remember

Think before you click, share, or post. Your personal information is valuable, so protect it.
