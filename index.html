Online Privacy

What is Online Privacy?

Online privacy is the protection of your personal information while using the internet. It means being careful about what information you share and knowing how websites and applications may collect and use your data.

Why is Online Privacy Important?

Protecting your privacy can help reduce the risk of identity theft, scams, unauthorized access, and misuse of personal information. Everyone should learn how to protect their information online.

Common Privacy Risks

  • Sharing too much personal information
  • Using weak passwords
  • Clicking suspicious links
  • Using unsafe websites
  • Giving unnecessary app permissions

How to Protect Your Privacy

  1. Use strong and unique passwords.
  2. Enable two-factor authentication.
  3. Check your privacy settings.
  4. Think before sharing personal information.
  5. Do not click suspicious links.
