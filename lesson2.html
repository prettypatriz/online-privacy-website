Protecting Your Personal Information

What Personal Information Should You Protect?

Personal information includes your name, phone number, email address, location, passwords, photos, and financial information.

How Can You Protect Yourself?

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Keep your passwords private.
  • Check your social media privacy settings.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Think before sharing personal information online.

Remember

Always think before you click, share, download, or post something online.
