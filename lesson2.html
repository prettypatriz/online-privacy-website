PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

Personal information includes your name, phone number, email address, location, passwords, photos, and financial information.

It is important to protect this information because sharing too much information online can put your privacy and security at risk.

WAYS TO PROTECT YOUR PRIVACY

  • Keep your passwords private.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Check your social media privacy settings.
  • Think before posting personal information.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.

REMEMBER

Think before you click, share, download, or post. Protecting your personal information is an important part of staying safe online.
